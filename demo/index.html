Avg-MSE vs random

Held-out unseen operators at the 25% budget, mean over 10 seeds. Left of the line is better.

What you are looking at. degenerate is a deliberate positive control: it spends the identical budget but concentrates it into a handful of operator×scene groups. You can see it clump, and it costs +7.2% Avg-MSE. That is how we know the harness detects a real diversity effect rather than noise — and therefore that the smaller margins above are measurements, not luck.

Avg-MSE is an offline proxy, not robot success — the EgoVerse authors use it on the same terms. One task, one lab; no claim of transfer. Full method, statistics and limitations in the repo.
